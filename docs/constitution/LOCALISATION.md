---
title: Localisation
description: Language policy for documentation and product copy
updated: 2026-08-08
---

- Harness documentation: English.
- User-facing product language: **English first** (`UI_EN.txt` and English
  phrase pools). Additional locale tables may ship under
  `media/lua/shared/Translate/<LOCALE>/` when the owner adds them.
- **Steam / engine configuration identifiers: ENGLISH ONLY** (hard) —
  see [[adr-06-steam-configurations]] and [[steam-language-policy]].
- Text and prose: recommended English; soft rule — you may adapt prose to
  your language (same ADR).
- Dates: ISO 8601 (`YYYY-MM-DD`).

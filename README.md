# True Detective

**Project Zomboid · Build 42.20 · playable profession**

*"Everybody in Knox County lost something. I just get paid to notice where it went."*

The **Detective** is not a soldier and not a ranger. He spent twenty years
reading rooms, faces, and the silence on the wrong side of locked doors. The
outbreak did not invent him; it only gave him a larger caseload and fewer
clients who could still pay.

*"You can keep the forest. I'll take the town."*

## The kit

He enters the world the same way he entered every scene he was hired for:

- a **fedora** and a **leather long coat** — colours vary; the silhouette holds
- a **loaded Patrol Revolver** and a **full box of .357** — never a pistol
- a **magnifying glass**, because the work depends on it

Some mornings he also has a **pipe**, **loose tobacco**, and a **lighter**, or
another lighter and a bottle of whiskey. No spare cigarette pack with the pipe.
Seventy-five percent on the optionals. Luck was never part of the job description.

*"Sometimes is all a man gets."*

## The method — Survey Sense

The glass is equipment, not a power switch. While it sits in either hand,
search gets **half again as sharp** — the urban eye for trash, junk, and spent
brass wakes up properly. Put the glass away and that edge goes with it.

What he *chooses* to do is simpler. Right-click **Investigate**.

On an **unalerted walker** or a **corpse**, he gives **one lead** out loud:
*his mate is still to the west*, *his pack comes from the kitchen* — or,
when nobody answers, a flat *he's alone*.

Doors are just doors. He does not probe them.

Walk with the glass and the ground may show **footprints** and a direction.
A miss stays quiet. He does not aim at houses to scan the block. He does not hold
a channel on the skyline. One subject, one whisper. A living partner already
marked with a lead will not be read again.

Without the glass the menu still shows — old reflex — and he will tell you he
needs the lens. He has six ways to put that.

Self-investigation is drafted, not finished.

## What he is good at

Trash, junk, junk weapons, ammunition, medical leftovers: the residue people
leave when they stop being careful. Strong vision in unlit buildings.
Weather still hurts him outside. Aiming **+2**, profession cost **−8** like the
Veteran — the gun is habit, not theatre.

*"Stay behind me. And give me a minute with the glass."*

## Install

1. Subscribe on the
   [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3383387174),
   or place the mod under `Zomboid/mods`.
2. Main menu → **Mods** → enable **True Detective** → Apply.
3. New game → pick the **Detective** occupation.

Local developers: `scripts/install-local.sh` rsyncs into `~/Zomboid/mods/TrueDetective`.

description=[h1]True Detective[/h1]
description=
description=[i]"Everybody in Knox County lost something. I just get paid to notice where it went."[/i]
description=
description=Build 42.20 playable profession: the [b]Detective[/b]. Not a soldier, not a ranger. Twenty years of reading rooms, faces, and locked doors — now the county is the caseload.
description=
description=[h2]The kit[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Fedora, leather long coat, trousers — colours vary.
description=[*] Loaded Patrol Revolver and a [b]full box of .357[/b]. Never a pistol.
description=[*] Magnifying glass. The work depends on it.
description=[*] Sometimes (75%): pipe + loose tobacco + lighter; also another lighter and/or whiskey. No cigarette pack.
description=[*] +2 Aiming at the Veteran's price (−8 points).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Survey Sense[/h2]
description=The glass is equipment, not a power switch. Hold it for [b]search ×1.5[/b]. Put it away and the edge goes with it.
description=
description=The only action you activate is right-click [b]Investigate[/b] on a quiet zombie or a corpse:
description=[list]
description=[*] Quiet (unalerted) zombie — [i]his mate is …[/i] or [i]his pack comes from …[/i], or he walks alone.
description=[*] Corpse — same voice, from the body.
description=[*] Walk with the glass — a chance to read footprints and a direction. A miss stays quiet.
description=[*] Doors are not a target.
description=[*] No glass? The menu still appears. He will say he needs the magnifying glass — six phrasings.
description=[/list]
description=
description=One subject, one lead. No aiming at houses, no skyline channel. Marked partners are not read twice.
description=
description=[h2]On the streets[/h2]
description=Urban forage: strong dark-room vision; affinity for trash, junk, junk weapons, ammunition, and medical finds. No forest specialty.
description=
description=[i]"Stay behind me. And give me a minute with the glass."[/i]
